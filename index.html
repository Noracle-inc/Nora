About Noracle

Noracle is built to listen without judgment. It helps users pause, reflect, and connect with healthier habits and support, while staying honest about the limits of AI care.

Why Noracle

People first

Safe by design

Noracle is AI, not a therapist. It is designed to encourage real human support and to be clear about the limits of an AI conversation.

You stay in control of whether you use Noracle. When you need support, connecting with a trusted person or qualified professional matters.

Designed around you

Human-centered

AI can support reflection, but it does not replace human care or connection. Noracle keeps your choices, privacy, and independence at the center.
